Smart Flood Detection with SMS Notification using GSM Module

A smart flood detection and alert system designed to detect rising water levels and provide an early warning through SMS notification. The system uses sensors to monitor the water level and a GSM module to send an alert message to the concerned person when the water reaches a predefined level.

The system also collects the location information of the detected flood condition, helping users identify the affected location and respond quickly during emergency situations.

Technologies: Arduino, Water Level Sensor, GSM Module, GPS/Location Module, Embedded Systems and IoT.
